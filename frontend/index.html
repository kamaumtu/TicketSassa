Frequently Asked Questions (Flights)

How do I receive my flight e-ticket after booking?

Once your M-Pesa STK Push or card payment is confirmed, your PNR booking reference and electronic ticket are sent immediately to your email and phone.

Can I pay for flights using M-Pesa on Ticketsasa?

Enter your M-Pesa number at checkout and approve the payment prompt on your phone.

What documents do I need for domestic vs international flights?

Domestic flights within Kenya accept a valid National ID or Passport. International flights require a passport valid for at least 6 months from your travel date.

What baggage allowance is included?

Each flight listing displays the included cabin and checked baggage allowance, and you can add extra 23kg luggage during checkout.
