03 SEP

September Events Radar

Events

A new month means new experiences, new connections, and plenty of reasons to step out of your routine. This September, the calendar is bringing together the thrill of horse racing, the roar of powerful engines, opportunities to sharpen your sales skills, and important conversations about the future of energy in Africa. Whatever your idea of a wonderful experience is, there is something waiting for you.

Sell The Way Customers Buy

Selling is not just about having a great product; it is about understanding the person making the decision. Starting Monday, 7th September Silicon Insights Global Consulting gives sales teams an opportunity to rethink and strengthen their sales approach. Running live on zoom the programme combines practical learning, a dedicated workbook and connection through WhatsApp community support. The focus? Learning how to align your sales process with the way customers decide to buy. If improving your sales game is on your September to-do list, this might be the investment your business needs.

Race Wars 2nd Edition

Some weekends are made for relaxing, 12th and 13th September are made for speed. Expect the roar of engines, impressive cars and the electrifying atmosphere that only comes when motorsport fans gather in one place. Whether you are passionate about cars or simply looking for an exciting weekend experience, Race Wars is the place to catch the action and soak the energy.

KMS Affordable Book Sale and Market

Book lovers are in for a treat at the KMS Affordable Book Sale & Market, hosted at The Courtyard inside the Nairobi National Museum. You can hunt for budget-friendly reads, search for rare collector titles, or meet fresh local authors at this family-friendly event. Plus, with artisanal vendors offering unique local goods, it is the ideal spot to shop, unwind, and enjoy a vibrant day out in Nairobi. Save the date for Saturday, 12th September 2026.

CrabMen Track Day

If burning rubber is more your speed, gear up for Crabmen Track Day taking over the Rift Valley Motorsports Club in Nakuru. Dubbed Kenya's first professional track day in history, the action-packed weekend features modified car exhibitions, high-speed drift practice sessions, tarmac track runs, and a head to head drift competition. Clear your schedule for 19th and 20th September 2026.

IEEE Power Africa Conference 2026

The future of Africa is being written, and Nairobi is set to be part of the conversation. From 21st September 2026, brilliant minds, industry leaders, innovators, and changemakers will come together at Safari Park Hotel. Under the theme "Accelerating Resilient Energy Systems & Industrial Decarbonization through Innovation," the conference will create a space for bold ideas, groundbreaking conversations and innovative solutions focused on building a more resilient and sustainable energy future for Africa.

The Coastal Fashion Brunch
• Date: Sunday, 20th September 2026 • Time: 12:00 PM • Venue: Saffron Gardens Malindi

Experience the ultimate blend of style, cuisine, and coastal ambiance. Indulge in an all-you-can-eat Sunday brunch, watch a stunning live fashion show on the runway, shop unique artisan products, and network with a vibrant crowd on the Kenyan coast. See you there!

Women Empowerment Summit 2026

Ready to elevate your leadership and business journey? The Women Empowerment Summit returns to Mombasa for a two-day deep dive into digital transformation, practical business strategies, and high-impact networking with visionary women leaders. Mark your calendars for September 25th at PrideInn Paradise Beach Resort, Shanzu, Mombasa.

Beyond The Bedside Nursing 3.0

Healthcare is evolving rapidly, and nursing is no longer confined to traditional settings. Beyond The Bedside 3.0 brings together expert leaders for honest, uncut panel discussions on big data, AI, research, entrepreneurship, and emerging niches. Whether you are a nursing student or a seasoned senior nurse, this event is designed to help you turn your dream career into reality. Save the dates for Saturday 26th September at Workable Nairobi, Sanlam Towers.

06 AUG

Your Ultimate August Event Guide

Events

Ready to upgrade your August plans? From family-friendly tech camps and outdoor kids' festivals to high-octane Motorsport races and soul nourishing conferences, this month is stacked with top-tier experiences. Dive into our curated guide below to find out where you need to be this August!

Rise to Dream - Chess & Coding Camp

Level up your child's problem-solving skills this month! The Rise to Dream Chess & Coding Camp combines critical thinking with hands-on tech. Tailored for ages pre-school and up with full dedicated support for neurodivergent kids through the autisticnetwork. This 5-day camp takes young minds from chess tactics to basic coding in a supportive, fun environment. Spaces are limited, secure your child's spot for August 10th - 15th.

Ballpoint Karaoke Night (August Edition)

Got a shower-singing voice ready for the spotlight? Ballpoint Karaoke night is back to kick off your weekend with immaculate vibes. Clear your schedule for Friday 14th August, step up to the mic, and get the weekend started right.

Good. Bad. Ugly

Prepare for an evening of pure, unfiltered human connection. A stellar lineup of storytellers such as Google Oputu, Paris Maleko, Reuben Kigame, Nyokabi Macharia, and Manya Prudie share personal triumphs, challenges, and heart-stirring reflections on stage.
